# cake-chaos
Cake Chaos 🍰☕
Welcome to Cake Chaos!
A fun and interactive game where you step into the shoes of a cake master. Your challenge? Assemble the perfect cakes by adding the right amount of ingredients—candles, berries, choco chips, and more—before time runs out! Speed and accuracy are your best friends as you race to fulfill as many orders as possible.

Features:
🎯 Random Orders: Each round comes with unique ingredient requirements.
⏱️ Beat the Clock: Finish orders in the least amount of time to score bonus points.
🎉 Engaging Gameplay: Add ingredients with a click and see your cake come to life!
📈 Score Tracker: Track your wins and strive for a high score.
🔄 Easy Reset: Start over anytime for a fresh challenge.

How to Play:
Check the order requirements for candles, berries, and choco chips.
Add ingredients by clicking the buttons.
Submit the order when your cake matches the requirements.
Earn points for each correct order and bonus points for speed!

Technologies Used:
React for dynamic UI interactions.
CSS for styling the cakes and UI.
JavaScript for game logic and state management.
